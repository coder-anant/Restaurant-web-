Our Story

A Legacy of
Culinary Mastery

Founded in 2011, Aurelian began as a vision to redefine fine dining. Chef Marco Valenti brings two decades of experience from the world's most prestigious kitchens in Paris, Tokyo, and New York.

Every ingredient is sourced from sustainable farms and artisan producers. Our menu evolves with the seasons, ensuring each visit offers a new chapter in your culinary journey.
